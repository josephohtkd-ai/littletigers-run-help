Get help

Write to us

Email josephohtkd@gmail.com for help, to report a problem with another player, or with any question about this game. Tell us what happened and which iPhone you use. Please leave out personal details about a child.

How do I play?

Swipe left or right to change lanes, swipe up to jump, swipe down to slide and tap to kick. You can replay the tutorial any time from Settings.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device. There is no account, so deleting the app also deletes your coins, belts, tigers and boards.

How do I restore a purchase?

Go to the Shop, open the board you bought and tap Restore. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments and refunds. Ask for one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Why can't I watch an ad for a second chance?

Ads need an internet connection and are not always available. Try again on your next run.

Someone on the leaderboard has a bad name. What can I do?

Open the Leaderboard and tap the flag on that player's row. Choose Report this name to hide it while we review it, or Block this player to remove them from your own view. Blocked players can be brought back with Unblock all at the bottom of the Leaderboard.

How do I change my name or leave the leaderboard?

Use Change name or Leave the leaderboard at the bottom of the Leaderboard. Leaving deletes your name and scores from our server straight away.

Privacy policy

Last updated 9 November 2026

Little Tigers Run is made by Joseph Oh ("we"). This policy explains what the game does with information, in plain words.

The short version

  • You can play without an account. We never ask for an email address, age or surname.
  • The online leaderboard is optional. Anything you enter there can be seen by everyone playing.
  • Ads play only if you choose to watch one, and they are not personalized.
  • We do not sell information about you.

What we handle, and why

Your game progress

What
Coins, belts, tigers, boards, missions, settings and your play statistics.
Why
To keep your game and adjust how runs play.
Where
Only on your device. It is not sent to us.
Kept
Until you delete the app.

Leaderboard details, if you join

What
The name you type, the country you pick, your tiger, board and belt, your best and weekly scores, your friend code and friends list, and a random player number.
Why
To rank players and show the boards.
Where
On our server. Your name, country, tiger, board, belt and scores are public. Please use a first name or nickname only.
Kept
Until you leave the leaderboard. A short record used to check each run for cheating is removed after a few hours.

Reports about names

What
The reported name, the time, and either the reporter's random player number or a scrambled form of their internet address.
Why
To review names and keep the leaderboard safe. We may hide or remove a name or a player.
Where
On our server. Only we can see reports.
Kept
Until the report is reviewed, or the player leaves. If we remove a name, we keep that name, with nothing linking it to the player, so nobody can use it again.

Internet address

What
Your device's internet address, when it connects to the leaderboard.
Why
To stop abuse, such as too many requests.
Where
Held briefly in memory on our server, and it may appear in short-term logs at our hosting provider.

Ads, through Google AdMob

What
Google's ad software runs in the iPhone app. It may receive device and app information, a general location worked out from your internet address, and how ads perform.
Why
To deliver the optional second-chance ad and measure it.
Settings
We mark every ad request as directed to children, so ads are not personalized. The app does not ask for permission to track you.
More
Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps.

Purchases

What
Apple processes payments. We only learn which item was bought. We never see your payment details.
More
Apple's privacy policy.

What the game does not do

  • It does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • It does not include other analytics or tracking services.

Children and parents

Many of our players are children. Playing the game does not require any personal information. A child should ask a parent or guardian before joining the leaderboard, because the name they type is public.

Parents and guardians can ask us to see, correct or delete their child's leaderboard details. Write to josephohtkd@gmail.com with the player's name and country.

Your choices

  • Delete your leaderboard details: choose Leave the leaderboard in the game. Your name and scores are removed from our server straight away.
  • Change your name: choose Change name in the Leaderboard.
  • Delete your progress: delete the app.
  • Ask us: write to josephohtkd@gmail.com to see or delete what we hold about you. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights, including the right to complain to your data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update it here and change the date at the top.
